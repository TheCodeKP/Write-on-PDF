Select text on the page to highlight, underline or strike it.

With any tool in hand, click something already on the page to pick it up. Drag and it draws instead.

With Select, click a note to select it, or drag a box over text notes (Ctrl+A for the whole page, Shift-click to add). Double-click a text note to type in it. Then align left, right, top or bottom, or change size for all of them.
